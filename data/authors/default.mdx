## About this site

RC Drift Parts publishes upgrade guides, parts comparisons, and setup tutorials for 1/10 drift platforms such as Tamiya TT-02, MST RMX, and YD-2. The goal is practical, tested advice—what to buy, how to install it, and how to tune it for consistent drift performance.

## Also by the same author

I also run [MeBitKit](https://mebitkit.com/), focused on DIY electronics and 2-axis servo kits for makers who want to build, wire, and learn hands-on. If you enjoy tinkering beyond the track—microcontrollers, custom servo setups, or starter hardware kits—you may find useful projects and guides there.
