## Why Five-Spoke CNC Wheels + KO2 Tires on a Crawler

### Scale look that still works on the trail

**Five-spoke** faces read like real truck wheels in photos and at the trail head—especially on **SCX10 II** and **TRX-4** bodies with open wheel openings. **CNC-machined or CNC-finished** rims often carry sharper spoke edges than basic molded wheels, which helps **scale realism** when you shoot low angles on rocks.

### KO2 tread for mixed terrain

**KO2-style** patterns (all-terrain knobbies) balance **bite on loose dirt** with **reasonable performance on rock and hardpack**. They are a practical default when you do not want a pure mud paddle or a street-smooth slick. Expect **some hum on pavement**—crawler tires are rarely silent on asphalt.

### 2.2" fitment ecosystem

Staying in the **2.2" crawler size class** makes it easier to compare against **stock SCX10 II** and many **aftermarket 2.2"** options. Jumping to a much larger outer diameter without lifting or trimming can cause **rubbing at lock** or **drag on links** under articulation.

## Fitment Checklist (Before You Mount)

### Chassis and driveline

- **Hex fit:** Wrong hex = instant return. Carry a **12 mm wrench** to the bench and trial-fit one wheel.
- **Offset / backspacing:** Aggressive offset can hit **steering links** or **chassis rails** at full turn.
- **Weight:** Heavier wheels change **center of gravity** slightly—usually minor on crawlers, but noticeable on **front-heavy hill climbs**.

### Body and suspension

- Measure **tire OD** at ride height with **shocks settled**. Compare to **inner fender** and **shock tower** clearance.
- Cycle **full droop and compression**—crawlers articulate more than touring cars; rub spots appear at extremes.
- If you run **larger tires**, plan for **pinion/ spur** stress; slow-speed torque climbs are harder on drivetrain when OD grows a lot.

### Honest compatibility note

Listings group **SCX10 II**, **TRX-4**, and **H8H** because they share the **1/10 crawler** parts culture—not because every sub-variant clears identical. **TRX-4 Sport vs Defender**, **SCX10 II Jeep vs pickup**, and **H8H wheelbase** can all differ in arch shape. **Test fit one corner first.**

## Install and Setup Tips

1. **Clean hex and hubs**—grit causes wobble that feels like bad tires.
2. **Snug wheel nuts** with a **thin-wall socket**; do not crush plastic hex adapters.
3. If tires are **unmounted to rims**, use proper **CA or tire glue** on the bead; let cure before water runs.
4. **Torque check after first crawl**—loose nuts are common after the first rocks session.
5. **Balance visual alignment**—all four should match OD; mismatched inserts foam can lean the body.

For **quick body access** after installs, magnetic mounts (see [CNC magnetic body mount](/guides/cnc-magnetic-body-mount-guide)) pair well with wheel swaps—you will open the shell more often while testing clearance.
